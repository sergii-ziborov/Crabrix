Crabrix is a Rust programming and learning environment. No account or sign-in is required. Rust source is visible and editable; compilation and execution use the bundled local compiler and bounded WASI runtime.

What is new in 1.2.1 (a small update to 1.2.0; no new features, permissions or data use):

1. Learn: the course list scrolls smoothly again when a scroll starts on a course card. Course cards still reveal Remove when swiped from right to left; scrolling closes an open card.

2. Learn → "Learn with your AI agent": a tidier layout. The intro is one card; the optional one-time feedback question has three buttons (it only opens an email draft the user sends themselves); setup steps for each AI client are numbered. Connect my progress shows three short points and a Connect button, with the full list of what is shared and what is never shared one tap away under "What is shared, and what never is"; the confirmation shown before anything is uploaded lists it in full, as in 1.2.0.

Connect my progress is unchanged from 1.2.0: optional and off until the user turns it on; the progress snapshot is encrypted on the device with a key that exists only on the device and in the personal URL shown to the user; crabrix.com stores only ciphertext and token hashes; Disconnect deletes the link and all data on crabrix.com. To review without an AI client: Learn → Learn with your AI agent → Connect → Connect and upload, see the personal URL with Copy/Share, then Disconnect. The app contains no AI model and sends no prompts or code.

Existing progress, answers, projects, Flashcards and Atlas recordings are retained after the update. Apart from the optional, user-initiated Connect my progress (encrypted, deletable at any time), the app sends no personal data; the rest of the app works offline.
