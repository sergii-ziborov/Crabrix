Privacy

Privacy Policy

No account. No analytics. No tracking. This page describes exactly what leaves your device, which is almost nothing.

LAST UPDATED 28 AUGUST 2026

The short version. Crabrix has no account and no analytics. Your code, your projects, and your progress stay on your device. One optional feature — the public leaderboard — sends a display name you choose and your rating, and only after you switch it on. You can delete that entry from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Crabrix is made by Serhii Ziborov, an independent developer. There is no company, no investor, and no third party with access to anything described on this page. Contact: support@crabrix.com.

What stays on your device

All of this is stored locally and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your Rust code and projects — files you write, import from GitHub, or open from the Files app.
  • Build output — compiler diagnostics, program stdout and stderr, and cached build artifacts.
  • Learning progress — lessons completed, answers, topic mastery, rating, achievements, health and energy.
  • Preferences — appearance, editor font size, and the rest of Settings.
  • Downloaded crates — package sources and compiled artifacts, cached so a project rebuilds offline.

Deleting the app removes all of it. Crabrix keeps no copy and has no way to recover it.

What leaves your device

1. Package downloads (crates.io)

When you add a dependency, Crabrix fetches it from the crates.io registry (index.crates.io and static.crates.io) over HTTPS. These are ordinary requests for public files. Crabrix sends no identifier with them and crates.io is operated by the Rust Foundation under its own privacy policy.

2. Repository import (GitHub)

If you import a public repository, Crabrix downloads its archive from github.com over HTTPS. Only the URL you typed is sent. No credentials are involved and no private repositories are accessed.

3. The public leaderboard — optional, off by default

In Profile you can switch on Publish my rating. Until you do, nothing is sent. When you do, each submission contains:

FieldWhat it is
Display nameThe name you type. It is not read from your device, your Apple Account, or anywhere else.
Player keyA random string generated on your device the first time you publish, stored in the Keychain. It is not derived from your device, your Apple Account, or any advertising identifier.
Rating and rankYour point total and its tier name.
CountsLessons completed, lines of Rust changed, achievements unlocked, best Code Recall level.

Nothing else is sent: no email address, no name from your Apple Account, no contacts, no location, no device identifier, no code, and no file names. The entry is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure and shown on the public leaderboard.

Deleting your leaderboard entry

Profile → Remove my entry from the board deletes the row immediately and permanently. Nothing on your device is affected. You can also write to support@crabrix.com and it will be removed for you.

What Crabrix never does

  • No tracking, in the App Store sense or any other. Your activity is not linked to data from other apps or websites.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no advertising networks.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting, or attribution SDK. None is linked into the app.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is nobody to sell it to and nothing to sell.
  • No access to your photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or health data. The app requests none of these permissions.
  • No reading of your code by anyone but you. It is compiled on your device by a compiler that ships inside the app.

Children

Crabrix is a programming tool suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. The optional leaderboard asks only for a display name; please do not enter a real name, a school, or any other identifying detail. Names are filtered and can be reported and removed — see Support.

Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for the leaderboard entry is your consent, given by switching the feature on, and withdrawn by removing the entry or turning it off. Because the entry contains only what you typed and numbers the app calculated, access, correction, and erasure are all available directly in the app: change the name and publish again, or remove the entry. For anything else, write to support@crabrix.com.

There is no data retention schedule to describe, because there is nothing else retained. A leaderboard entry stays until you delete it.

Changes to this policy

If Crabrix ever collects something new, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. The app's App Store privacy details are kept in step with this page.
